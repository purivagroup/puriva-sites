About Puriva

Quality products, built around everyday life.

Puriva Group, operating through Puriva Ultracare Products Limited, is a Nigerian-owned diversified consumer-products company focused on the development, manufacturing, distribution, wholesale and retail of quality everyday products.

Our business is centred on creating and delivering reliable products that support cleanliness, hygiene, personal care and improved everyday living.

At Puriva, we believe that quality products should be accessible, dependable and designed around the everyday needs of consumers.

We are building a business founded on quality, innovation, operational excellence, integrity and customer satisfaction. Through strategic partnerships, responsible production practices and an expanding distribution network, we aim to serve households, retailers, wholesalers and business partners across Nigeria and beyond.

Our areas of focus include

  • Cleaning and hygiene products
  • Liquid detergents
  • Powder detergents
  • Personal care products
  • Household and everyday consumer products

Our Commitment
